07 / LET'S CLEAR THINGS UP

Myth vs reality.

There are plenty of misconceptions about Computer Science. Let's separate the myths from what is actually true.

MYTH 01

"Computer Science is only about coding."

REALITY

It also includes data, people, design, networks, security, theory and much more.

MYTH 02

"You must know everything before you start."

REALITY

Every expert was once a beginner. Learning happens one step at a time.

MYTH 03

"Computer Scientists only work with computers."

REALITY

They help solve problems in healthcare, education, transport, finance and many other fields.
